Terms & Conditions

Plain-language terms for this academic demonstration website.

1. Academic prototype

Lumière Beauty is a student web-development prototype. Products, prices, accounts, payments and delivery tracking are demonstrations and do not create a real commercial transaction.

2. Accounts and browser storage

Demo account, cart, wishlist, review and order information is stored locally in the browser. Do not enter real passwords or sensitive payment information.

3. Payments

The site records only a preferred demo payment method. It never requests card numbers, CVV codes or banking credentials.

4. Delivery tracking

Delivery stages and maps are simulated for assessment. They do not represent a real courier location.

5. Returns and privacy

The displayed 30-day returns message is part of the prototype design. Contact and enquiry forms may be submitted to Netlify Forms when the site is deployed and configured.
