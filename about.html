Our story

Made for every face

Lumière started in a Durban kitchen in 2019, mixing shades for friends who could never find a match.

Shade-first, always

We build every base product in thirty shades from the start — never a pale launch with deeper tones added later once the reviews come in.

Formulas that behave

Humid coastal mornings are our testing ground. If it slides, creases or oxidises here, it doesn't ship.

Kind by default

Cruelty-free across the range, with refillable compacts on powders and palettes, and recyclable outer cartons.
